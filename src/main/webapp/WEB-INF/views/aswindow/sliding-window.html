Sliding Window

We have emerged as a leading Sliding window manufacturer in India. Our double glazed sliding windows are very popular fixture in homes and offices purpose as they are very easy and convenient to use. They are also cost-effective as they are made of durable material and require very low maintenance.

These Double glazed sliding windows have one fixed pane and one sliding pane, which rolls on a track inside the window frame to open or close. They have dual seals and take a 22mm double glazed unit. The maximum size for these sliding windows is 1200 mm wide x 1600 mm high. Our sliding window range are very popular for many uses as it provides modern clean lines and is ideal for areas where space is limited. Additionally our upvc sliding windows boast secure multi-locking hardware, toughened glass and effortless sliding action. A separate sliding track can be added for a sliding fly screen.
  • One,two and three track sliding, window and fly screen options.
  • Multi-chamber profile design, frame depth from 62 to 112 mm.
  • 10 mm sash overlap for enhanced performance.
  • Internal glazing options from 4 mm single to 22 mm double.
  • There is a Choice of single or multi-point locking devices.
